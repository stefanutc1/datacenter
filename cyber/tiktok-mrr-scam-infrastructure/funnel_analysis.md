## 1. Algorithmic Exploitation Mechanics

The primary driver of the Master Resell Rights (MRR) phenomenon on TikTok is the exploitation of high-velocity organic discovery algorithms.

Threat actors deploy "faceless" content creation pipelines characterized by:
1. **Automated B-Roll Stock Video**: 5 to 7-second looped clips of luxury vehicles, minimalist workspaces, or aesthetic cityscapes sourced from royalty-free repositories (Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay).
2. **Synthetic Voiceover Synthesis**: Realistic text-to-speech audio generated via ElevenLabs or built-in CapCut TTS engines reading emotionally manipulative scripts designed to trigger financial anxiety and FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
3. **High-Contrast Text Hooks**: Overlay captions formatted with bold typography (e.g., *"How I made $4,850 in 3 days using only my phone"*).
4. **High-Frequency Multi-Account Swarms**: Running 5-10 accounts per operator, uploading 3-5 clips per day per account to maximize algorithmic lottery probability.

```
[ TikTok App (In-App Webview) ]

   v
[ Stan.store Landing Page Architecture ]
   |-- High-conversion one-page UI
```
