## 1. Textual Forensics & Token Analysis

Textual extraction of the delivered PDF deliverables revealed distinct structural fingerprints characteristic of unedited LLM output:

1. **Standardized Prompt Chaining Artifacts**:
   * Headers and bullet points follow uniform three-sentence explanatory structures.
   * Universal presence of default generative transition phrases:
     * *"In today's fast-paced digital era..."*
     * *"It is crucial to emphasize that consistency is key..."*
     * *"Let us dive deeper into the world of digital marketing..."*
2. **Absence of Concrete Operational Telemetry**:
   * Zero references to actual API endpoints, advertising budget allocation formulas, conversion rate optimization (CRO) testing frameworks, or web development code.
   * Descriptions of "SEO", "Email Marketing", and "Affiliate Marketing" are restricted to superficial, high-level dictionary definitions.
