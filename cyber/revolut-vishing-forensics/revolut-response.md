Revolut Security Warning: How to Protect Yourself from Vishing and Scams
Voice phishing, or 'vishing', is a form of impersonation scam where criminals call you pretending to be an authority figure, such as a Revolut employee, police officer, or IT technician. The call is designed to induce extreme panic and trick you into performing high-risk actions.
To keep your account and personal information secure, please remember the following rules:
• We will never call you out of the blue: Revolut will never call you without booking the call and confirming it with you in advance.
• Keep your codes private: Never read a one-time passcode (OTP) or share PINs and passwords over the phone or chat. Real Revolut employees will never ask for these codes.
• Reject 'safe accounts': We will never ask you to move your money to a new or 'safe' account to protect it. If anyone tells you to do this, hang up immediately.
• Do not allow remote access: Never download software at the request of a caller. These tools allow criminals to control your device and access your accounts.
• Verify through the app: Support calls from us are carried out directly within the Revolut app. You can also use the in-app chat to confirm if a contact is legitimate.
• Hang up and wait: Scammers can hold the line open on landlines. Wait at least 10 minutes or use a different phone if you need to make a call after a suspicious interaction.
If you are ever unsure, stop all communication, freeze your cards in the Revolut app, and reach out to our Support team via the in-app chat.
